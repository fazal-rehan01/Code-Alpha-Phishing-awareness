Resources and cheat sheets

Print this page for a one-page reminder, or copy the templates into your own notes.

Cheat sheet: spotting a phishing message

  • Read the full sender address, not just the display name
  • Ask whether you were expecting this message
  • Hover over links and check where they really go
  • Treat urgency, threats and secrecy as warnings
  • Never enter credentials through a link in a message
  • Confirm money and data requests out of band
  • Check for double extensions on attachments
  • When in doubt, stop and ask

Cheat sheet: reading a URL

https://The connection is encrypted. This does not prove the site is honest.
support.paypal.com.account-check.netThe real domain is account-check.net. "support" and "paypal.com" are only subdomains.
paypa1.comThe digit 1 replaces the letter l. A classic look-alike.
paypal-secure-login.comHyphenated additions such as -secure or -login are a common giveaway.
http://No encryption at all. Any site asking for details over plain http is suspect.
